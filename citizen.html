Welcome to VIMS Public Services

The Vehicle Intelligence & Movement Tracking System (VIMS) provides transparent public services to verify registered vehicle status, register stolen vehicle alerts, and report cloned plate e-challan discrepancies directly to law enforcement command.

Citizen Advisory Guidelines
  • If you receive an automated traffic e-challan for a location where your vehicle was not present, immediately file a Cloned Plate Report.
  • Vehicles reported stolen are synchronized with automated junction cameras across the municipal boundary.
  • Official VIMS status indicators are derived directly from the national transport registry.
